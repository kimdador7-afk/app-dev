# Helluva Boss

Created by **Vivienne "VivziePop" Medrano**, this animated web series follows the misadventures of I.M.P. (Immediate Murder Professionals), an assassination company operating out of Hell. Their business plan is ~~terrible~~ highly effective: killing living people on Earth for vengeful sinners. 

> "When you want somebody gone, and you don't want to wait too long..." 
> — I.M.P. Commercial Jingle

---

## Immediate Murder Professionals (I.M.P.)

The chaotic team responsible for hits in the living realm consists of:

* **Blitzø:** The arrogant but deeply insecure founder (the 'o' is silent).
* **Moxxie:** The neurotic weapons specialist.
* **Millie:** The cheerful, hyper-violent bruiser and Moxxie's wife.
* **Loona:** The apathetic, phone-obsessed hellhound receptionist.

### Earth Access Protocol

To travel to the living world, the imps rely on the `Grimoire`, an ancient book of spells belonging to the Goetia demon, Prince Stolas.

## Explored Rings of Hell

While sinners are confined to the Pride Ring, hellborn creatures can travel. The show frequently explores these rings:

1. **Pride Ring:** Home to Imp City and I.M.P. Headquarters.
2. **Wrath Ring:** A rough-and-tumble, country-western landscape (Millie's home).
3. **Greed Ring:** A polluted mobster paradise home to cheap knock-off theme parks.
4. **Lust Ring:** A neon-drenched metropolis ruled by Asmodeus.
5. **Sloth Ring:** A medical hub where the Belphegor hospital is located.
6. **Gluttony Ring:** A massive, endless party hosted by Queen Bee-lzebub.
